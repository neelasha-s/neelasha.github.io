Projects
  • White-Box Steering Defenses for Safe AI
    I experimented with probe-gated activation steering methods to find the tradeoff between prompt injection attack suppression and benign task performance. I found that blind steering reduces attack success from 93% to 3%, whereas probe-gated steering is ineffective because it doesn't steer the entire attack span.
    Code · Write-up
  • Collusion and AI Control
    I replicated the passive collusion results from "When can we trust untrusted monitoring?" (Gardner-Challis, et al.) achieving a collusion ROC-AUC score of 0.98. My extension was a causal belief-flip experiment to isolate the relationship between self-recognition and collusion.
